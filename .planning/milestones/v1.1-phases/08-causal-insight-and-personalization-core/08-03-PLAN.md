| T-08-12 | T | Correction submission flow | mitigate | Require explicit confirmation state and validate target parameter/value ranges before persist. |
| T-08-13 | R | Correction auditability | mitigate | Persist correction timestamp, type, and applied influence metadata; expose confirmation receipts in UI state. |
| T-08-14 | I | Explainability detail surfaces | mitigate | Render only aggregated factors/weights and recency windows, not raw biometric sample lists or identifiers. |
